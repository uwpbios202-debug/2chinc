How to pay your rent

Step by step on your phone: sign in, connect your bank, and pay.

For your phone: iPhone or Android. It takes about 5 minutes the first time. After that, paying takes 30 seconds.

  • 1. Sign in
  • 2. Connect your bank
  • 3. Autopay
  • Zelle
  • Need help

Part 1. Sign in

  1. Open the website

    On your phone, open your web browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android). Go to 2ndchinc.org/resident. If you were told to register first, go to 2ndchinc.org/register and type the code from your notice.

    Save it to your home screen so it is one tap next time. iPhone: tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen.
  2. Type your email and tap "Email me a code"

    Use the email you gave us. If it is not the right one, tap Update your info here on that page and use your notice code.

  3. Get the 6-digit code from your email

    Open your email app. Find the message that says "is your 2nd Chance Housing sign-in code". If you do not see it, look in Spam or Junk. The code works for 10 minutes.

    iPhone tip

    Your keyboard may show the code above the letters. Tap it and it fills in for you.

    Android tip

    Press and hold the code in your email, tap Copy, then paste it in the box.

  4. Type the code and tap "Sign in"

    You will see your balance and your account number.

Part 2. Connect your bank and pay

You can pay with your debit card or from your bank account. Debit cards only (a credit card is refunded), in your name or a tenant on your lease. Tick save and next time it is just choose and pay. There is no fee to you. Stripe, a payment company used by many banks, keeps your bank login private. We never see it.

  1. Tap "Payments", then "Pay now"

    Look at the amount. It should be your rent. You can change it if you are paying a different amount.

  2. A secure Stripe page opens. Type your name and choose your bank.

    Type your full name in the first box. Then tap your bank in the list, or type its name in Search for your bank (for example Chase, Wells Fargo, U.S. Bank, Associated Bank).

  3. Sign in to your bank

    Use the same username and password you use for your bank app. This happens inside the bank's own secure window. Follow the steps on the screen to pick the checking account you want to pay from and to agree.

    Do not give anyone your bank password, not even us. You only type it into your bank's window on the Stripe page.
  4. Tap the blue "Pay" button

    It is at the bottom of the Stripe page. You come back to our website. Bank payments take 3 to 5 business days to finish. We email you a receipt, and the Payments tab will say "processing" and then "paid".

    No late fee while your bank payment is on its way. If you pay your rent on time, the payment is still waiting to clear, and it covers the rent you owe, we do not add a late fee while it clears. (If the bank later returns the payment, the late fee is added that day.)
I do not use online banking

You do not need online banking. On the Stripe page choose Card and type your debit card, or, to pay from your account, tap Enter bank details manually (see the next box) and type the routing number and checking account number for the account your debit card is tied to. These are not the long number on the card. Ask your bank for them: call the number on the back of your card, visit a branch, or look at a check from that account. When Stripe sends its two tiny deposits, your bank can tell you the amounts over the phone or at a branch. Banks will also set up free online banking for you if you ask. Need help? Call or text (262) 236-5443.

My bank is not on the list, or I do not want to sign in to my bank

Under the list of banks, tap Enter bank details manually (Stripe says it may take 1 to 2 business days). Type your routing number and account number. They are printed at the bottom of your checks, and in your bank app under Account details. Then:

  1. Stripe will say it will send two tiny deposits (pennies) to your account in 1 to 2 business days.
  2. When they arrive, Stripe emails you a link. Open it and type the two amounts. Your account is then connected.
  3. Come back to Payments and tap Pay now again.

Rent is still due on time while you wait. Use Zelle below that month if you need to.

Part 3. Autopay (optional)

Autopay pays your rent from your bank on the 1st of every month. You never forget, and you never pay a late fee. You can turn it off any time.

  1. Go to Payments and tap "Set up autopay"
  2. Connect your bank

    Do the same steps as Part 2: pick your bank, sign in, choose your checking account, agree. You will come back to our site and see "Autopay is on".

  3. To stop it

    Go to Payments and tap Turn off.

Paying with Zelle instead

  1. Open Payments and find the Zelle box

    It shows the Zelle email to send to, the amount, and your account number. Tap Copy next to each one.

  2. Open your bank's app and choose Zelle

    Send the amount to the Zelle email. In the memo (note), paste your account number. That is how we know the money is yours.

    Zelle is free and usually arrives in 1 to 2 days. It only works inside your bank's app.
Stuck? We will fix it.

Tap Report a problem at the bottom of the screen. Tell us what happened, and add a screenshot if you can. We read every one. Or call or text (262) 236-5443.
