If rent isn't paid: what happens, day by day

This is a sample of how a missed rent payment moves along, from rent due at 11:59 pm on the 1st to the sheriff. It shows how long you have to ask for help. Real dates are set by the court, so use these as a guide.

  1. Turn on JavaScript to see the dates for the next month. In short: rent is due on the 1st, the pay-or-vacate notice is mailed on the 2nd, the notice ends 5 days later, then a court case, a court date about two weeks after filing, and the sheriff about 10 days after a writ.

Keep your certified pay-or-vacate notice. It is what agencies ask for.

The pay-or-vacate (5-day) notice is mailed to you by certified mail. It states the exact rent owed and the exact last day to pay. Take it (the paper copy or the emailed copy) to the agency you are asking for help. Most programs want it as proof of the amount owed and the deadline.

  • Ask for help before the notice ends. Tell the agency the date it ends so they can move quickly.
  • The notice covers rent only, not late fees. Paying that full amount before it ends stops this process.
  • 2nd Chance Housing does not provide funds. You can show your rent ledger from your resident portal, and we provide the notice.
  • Some tenancies (for example, some leases longer than a year or Section 8 tenancies) get a 30-day notice. Your notice says which.

This sample is for information, not legal advice. For free legal help, see Mediation & legal help.

What to bring when you apply for help

Having these ready can cut days off the process:

  • Photo ID for every adult in the home
  • Your lease
  • Proof of income for the last 30–60 days, or proof that you lost income
  • Any notice you received, plus a rent ledger showing what you owe
  • A recent We Energies bill

2nd Chance residents: we do not provide funds. To show a program what you owe, print your rent ledger or show it on screen from your resident portal. If the program asks for a notice, we provide the 5-day notice or the pay or vacate notice. Email info@2ndchinc.org or call (262) 236-5443.
