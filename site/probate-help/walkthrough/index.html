Before we start, gather these.

  1. A certified copy of the death certificate, or the details from it.
  2. The recorded deed that shows who owns the property, and each parcel number, called a P I N.
  3. Names, addresses and phone numbers for every child, and for a surviving spouse if there is one.

The steps · 24 of 62

If a T O D I was recorded.

Review it

For each parcel, confirm the instrument covers it, names the right beneficiaries, and was recorded before her death. A T O D I on one parcel says nothing about another.

Record the death affidavit

A beneficiary may file a notice of death affidavit with the Clerk's Recordings office to confirm title. It is signed before a notary. Filing it is not a condition of the transfer.

Then: write the recorded document number in the note and go straight to the closing steps. Counsel still checks anything outside the instrument.

The steps · 43 of 62

If you chose a route other than full probate.

Heirship only

A court order that names the heirs. It does not appoint anyone, pay a lien, or settle a dispute. Get the title company's written requirements first.

Summary administration

Only if the gross estate is $100,000 or less and every heir consents in writing, with other conditions. The page tests the limit against your Assets card.

No estate case

Ask the title company in writing whether it will insure the transaction, and what it requires. Record the answer.

A dispute

Competing deeds or disagreements are not solved by an inheritance filing. Take the evidence to counsel.

Deliver the original will to the Clerk of the Circuit Court of Cook County (Probate Division)

Illinois law requires whoever holds the will to file it with the Clerk immediately after the death. Filing the will itself has no fee. Write who held the original, and the date it was filed, in the note.

755 ILCS 5/6-1Copy of Will (CCP 0317)
To do▾target mm/dd/yyyy

What to do

  1. Whoever holds the original must file it with the Clerk right away. Holding it back is a crime.
  2. Filing it has no fee.
  3. Write who held it and the date filed in the note, and set the date in the Timeline card.
