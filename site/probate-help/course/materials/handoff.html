7. Sample cover memo (fictional)

To: [Counsel]   From: [Researcher], 2nd Chance Housing   Date: [date]
Re: Marsh, 4410 S Example Ave, Chicago (FICTIONAL): package for your review

1. What this is. A package prepared with the family's written permission, for your review. Nothing has been filed or sent to any court. We are not a law firm and give no legal advice.

2. What is unresolved (please read first). Q1: Naomi Marsh's address is unknown. Q2: a 2023 church certificate names a possible spouse; the death certificate says widowed. Q3: deed says “Delia A. Marsh,” certificate says “Delia Ann Marsh.” Q4: a 2009 mortgage has no recorded release. Q5: PIN 20-00-000-002-0000 is deeded jointly with Robert Marsh (d. 2011). The heirs list is not marked complete.

3. What we found. Delia Ann Marsh died 03/14/2026 (certified certificate, C01). Recorder search 04/05/2026 for both PINs (index through 04/03/2026), results in C02 and C03.

4. What we ask. Please confirm the route and tell us which items to correct or add. Known dates: none.

5. Where things are. Files 01 to 06 and C01 to C12; originals register attached. All drafts are marked DRAFT.
