B. Unresolved legal and policy questions for counsel and leadership

  1. Is the researcher role (finding families, collecting records, typing facts, printing draft affidavits and worksheets) within what a non-lawyer organization may do? (705 ILCS 205/1; the system already says it is a checklist.)
  2. Approve, correct or replace the draft consent form: retention period, sharing with counsel and title companies, minors and persons with a disability, a witness or notary, applicable Illinois privacy rules.
  3. Is door-to-door and obituary-based outreach to bereaved families acceptable, and are there local solicitation or disclosure rules?
  4. 2nd Chance's rule is email for ordinary communication and mail only for legal notices. Are printed cards, door notes and leave-behind sheets allowed for outreach? (Leadership decision; the course assumes the organization issues an approved card.)
  5. Representative-capacity acknowledgment wording on the deed (open in the review package).
  6. Exact wording of “do not sign” guidance and the plan facts, and whether any should be softened or expanded.
  7. Who the researchers' supervisor and escalation contact are, and the organization's emergency steps for a person in crisis.

C. Missing inputs

  • Approved consent form, organization card or leave-behind (if allowed), ID badge policy.
  • Names and phone numbers of the supervisor and the counsel who receive packages.
  • Staff accounts and access-list setup for each researcher (the page is staff only, with two-step sign-in).
  • A signed-in review of the live case page to confirm the screens.
  • Narration audio: the deck uses the browser's own voice unless clips are recorded from docs/course-narration.txt.
  • A real-case pilot with one researcher and a supervisor, to test pacing.
