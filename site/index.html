A Home for a Fresh Start

2nd Chance Housing provides safe, stable homes for people and families facing barriers to housing, with mentoring and support for lasting independence. We do not conduct background checks—we focus on where you are today and where you want to go.

Available Units

Every home comes with mentoring and reentry support. We are accepting applications now for the units marked "Accepting applications", even while they are still being made ready. Applying is free.

Our Mission
