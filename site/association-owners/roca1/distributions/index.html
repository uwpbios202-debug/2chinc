Rent deductions and owner statements

For every condo unit 2nd Chance leases, each month: rent collected, minus what the unit owes ROCA1 (fines and fees first, then special assessments, this month's dues, then older balance — Rules & Regulations 2024 §11), minus the manager's fee in that unit's management agreement, = net to the owner. Property taxes are not deducted.

Two bank accounts. Rent is in 2nd Chance Housing's account; dues belong to ROCA1. Approving a statement changes nothing on the owner's account. Only when you record the transfer to ROCA1 (with its bank reference) are the payments posted to the owner's association ledger.

Pay owners (rent paid straight into ROCA1's account)

When a tenant's rent for a unit like 16 clears, what the owner owes ROCA1 is applied to her association account automatically. The rest is the owner's: send it by Zelle, then click Sent and enter the date and reference. Unit 15's owner withdraws his portion from ROCA1's account himself: give him the printed statement (Print statement) and click Withdrawn when it shows on the bank account.

Management agreement terms by unit

From each unit's signed Property Management Agreement. Units not listed use 0%. The 2020 ROCA1 PMA template: 0% management fee and a 3.0% collection fee only on card payments; the March 2020 Unit 1 agreement (prior owner): 3.1% and a $150 operating reserve. The Unit 16 agreement (7/24/2023) is encrypted and hasn't been read. For rent paid into ROCA1's account, the fee taken from each tenant payment is Mgmt % of every payment plus Stripe % of payments made through Stripe (none on check, money order or Zelle). Unit 15: 3.5% of every payment (Mgmt %), to cover Stripe and administrative fees (11/10/2026).
