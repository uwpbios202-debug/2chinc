Renewals

Leases for a term: set the renewal rent and send the offer (the tenant accepts and signs online, then the agent countersigns), or send a non-renewal notice. Month-to-month: change the rent with at least 28 days' notice. You get a digest on the 1st and 15th of leases ending within 90 days; tenants get reminders 7 and 14 days after an unanswered offer.
