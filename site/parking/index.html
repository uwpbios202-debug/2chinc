Rule 20. NO PARKING only when the system has proof that no payment was attempted: Revoke appears only then, and NO PARKING lifts by itself the moment a payment is started, reported, received or clearing (the office is emailed). Tenant units: the tenant's unpaid rent only (no late fees or fines; never the owner's dues), and not before 9 days after the rent was charged (the 10th). Parking tags (mirror hang tags)

Each unit has 2 red resident tags (12A, 12B; the letter is only for inventory) and 2 blue guest tags (codes G12 and G12B, both printed "GUEST 12"). A blue tag is the guest pass; a unit can lend one to another unit for up to 14 days, recorded on their Parking tab. A tag counts only once it is handed out: until then Always Towing's page says the unit has no tags yet and checks the plate only. After you hand them out, the tenant or owner taps "I received my tags" on their Parking tab. A tag marked lost or retired shows on Always Towing's page as not valid; when you mark one lost you can charge the $25 replacement fee (tenant ledger, or the owner's association account if there is no lease). To replace a lost tag, print a new one (e.g. 12C), add it below and hand it over. At move-out the unit's tags show Collect.
