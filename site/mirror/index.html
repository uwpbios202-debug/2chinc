The Human Mirror

Discover yourself. Define your future. Build your life.

Before anyone talks about programs or paperwork, the Mirror starts with you: who you are today, where you've been, what you're good at, and the life you want.

Starts with you

You answer in your own words, by speaking or typing. Skip anything. Come back any time. There are no wrong answers.

Strengths first

Your skills, experience and talents count as much as what you need. Nobody here is a list of problems.

Your plan, your choice

You decide the goals. A coordinator or Housing Navigator helps you find the people and programs to get there.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants a clear picture of where they stand and where they're going. It was first built with people looking for a stable home in mind, but housing is only one part of a life. The Mirror covers 15 areas, from health and work to relationships, purpose and what you give back.

How to start

  1. Get an invitation. For now, people join through a coordinator or Housing Navigator who works with 2nd Chance Housing. They add you and you get an email with the steps.
  2. Sign in with a code. No password to remember: we email you a 6-digit code each time.
  3. Start your Mirror. A few open questions at a time, at your own pace.
