About

The first thing we hand someone is a mirror

Not an application for assistance. A mirror.

Every person has a present, a past and a future. Every person has abilities, needs, preferences and something to give. Before we decide what someone needs or which programs they qualify for, we first try to understand the human being.

Not "the homeless person." Not "the unemployed person." The person.

What changes when you look in the mirror

As Michael Jackson sang in "Man in the Mirror," making the world a better place starts with taking a look at yourself. The Mirror starts there too, and then helps you make the change.

Someone might first say: "I'm 38. I don't have a job. I don't have money. I don't have anywhere to go."

After the Mirror, we might learn that she finished two years of college, is an excellent cook, raised two children, has managed people, enjoys working with older adults, wants to become a nurse, has always wanted to own a home, and wants to help other women who are where she has been.

She still needs a home. She also needs a path to nursing school, steady work and, one day, a home of her own. We're no longer only solving a housing problem. We're helping a person build the life she wants.

What we hold to

  • Dignity. Nobody is a collection of problems. Strengths count as much as needs.
  • Your choice. You set the goals. Nobody writes your vision for you.
  • Strengths. What you know, what you've survived and what you're good at are part of the plan.
  • Independence. The aim is a life you run yourself, with help only where you want it.
  • Continuity. Your Mirror stays yours. You can update it any time, and the story keeps going.
  • Contribution. Raising a family, mentoring, caring for someone or creating something all count.

Who is behind it

The Human Mirror is a project of 2nd Chance Housing Inc in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Coordinators and Housing Navigators use it with the people they serve, and the people own what they write.
