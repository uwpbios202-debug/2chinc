Can't sign in?

There's no password to forget

Each time you sign in, we email you a 6-digit code. Here's what to do when that doesn't work.

The code didn't arrive

  • Wait a minute, then look in your spam or junk folder.
  • Check the email address you typed. It must be the one your coordinator has for you.
  • Ask for a new code. Only the newest code works, and each code works for a short time only.

The email has a "Sign in" button

You can press it instead of typing the code. It opens the sign-in page in your browser.

I can't get into my email any more

Your Mirror is safe. Contact your coordinator or Housing Navigator, or 2nd Chance Housing Inc at (262) 236‑5443. We'll confirm who you are, in person or by phone, and then change the email address on your account. We never change it because of an email or text alone.

I think someone else is using my account

Call us right away at (262) 236‑5443. We'll close every open sign-in and check who looked at your Mirror.
