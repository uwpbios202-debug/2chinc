Fifteen areas of a life

Housing is one part of a life, not the whole of it. The Mirror looks at all fifteen.

For each area you can say how things are now, what you already have, what you're good at, what you'd like to change, and what would help. You choose which areas to start with; many people begin with the one that's on their mind today.
