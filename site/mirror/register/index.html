The Human Mirror is a private place for your whole life: your needs, your strengths and the plan you choose.

How to accept

  1. Sign in with the email address the invitation went to. We send you a 6-digit code; there is no password.
  2. On your Mirror, you see who invited you. Choose Accept or Decline.
  3. Nothing about you is shared with that organization until you accept, and you choose what they may see.
