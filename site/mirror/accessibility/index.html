Accessibility

Made to be easy to use

The Mirror should work for everyone, on any phone or computer, whatever your reading, sight, hearing or movement.

What's built in

  • Speak instead of type. Every answer box takes speech: the microphone on your phone's keyboard, or the Speak button on a computer.
  • Listen instead of read. Pages and questions have a Read aloud button.
  • Larger text. Press "Larger text" at the top of any page. The site remembers your choice on that device.
  • Plain words and short steps. Every question can be skipped and answered later.
  • Phone first. Every page works on a small screen, with large buttons that are easy to tap.
  • Keyboard. Everything can be reached with the Tab key, and you can always see where you are on the page.
  • Screen readers. Pages use proper headings, labels and announcements.
  • Strong contrast in light and dark mode, and no flashing or moving parts if your device asks for less motion.

Need help, or something doesn't work?

Tell your coordinator, or contact 2nd Chance Housing Inc: info@2ndchinc.org, (262) 236‑5443. Tell us the page and what happened, and we'll fix it or find another way for you to answer. Someone can also go through the questions with you in person or by phone.
