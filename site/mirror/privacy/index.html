What we keep

We keep only what you choose to tell us: your answers, your goals and plan, your Daily Number figures, and, later, documents you choose to upload.

You can skip any question. We only ask for what the Mirror needs.

Some documents, like an ID, can hold numbers that thieves want, such as a driver’s license number. Upload one only if you want to.

We keep your Mirror until you ask us to delete it.

Why we keep it

We use it to give you a clear picture of your life, to build the plan you choose, and to connect you with programs and people who can help.

We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it
  • You see everything in your Mirror.
  • Your coordinator or Housing Navigator sees your everyday answers (who you are, your strengths, your plans). This starts after you accept their organization’s invitation. You can stop working with them at any time.
  • Private topics are health, mental health, safety, legal matters, immigration, money details and ID papers. They stay hidden from your coordinator unless you share that topic.
  • Other organizations see nothing unless you agree to a referral. Then they see only what the referral needs. You can take a referral back at any time. After that we stop sharing. We cannot undo what they already received, so ask them about their rules.
  • Our staff. Only staff who need access to run the Mirror can reach the system. They can open a private topic only to fix a problem or keep it safe.
  • We keep a log. Every time someone opens a private topic, changes an answer, or is turned away, we write it down. You can see who did it, when, and what they did. We review the log every month.
  • Your earlier answers stay private. When you change an answer, we keep the old ones for you only. Organizations see only your current answer. You can also clear an answer so nobody sees one.

When we may have to share it

We do not share your private information to be nosy or for money. But the law can require us to share some information. Here are the cases we know about.

Reports about children

Wisconsin law requires some professionals to report if they have reason to suspect that a child they see in their work has been abused or neglected. Some of the staff who work with you may be in that group. If so, they must make that report, even if you ask them not to. Ask your coordinator if you are unsure.

Court orders and subpoenas

A court order is signed by a judge. A subpoena is different. In a court case, a lawyer can issue one. We will not give out your records just because someone asks.

If we get a court order or a subpoena for your records, our lawyer reviews it. We share only what the law requires. When we can, we ask the court to limit the request. We tell you first, unless the law or your safety says we cannot.

Emergencies

If we believe someone is in immediate danger, we may call 911 or share only what is needed to keep people safe.

Safety mode and shared devices

If someone may be watching your phone or email, turn on safety mode in Settings.

  • The Mirror never reads private questions out loud.
  • The Mirror checks with you before it saves a copy on your device.

On a shared or public device

  • Sign out when you finish, and close the browser window.
  • A file you download stays on that device until you delete it.
  • Your sign-in code comes by email. Anyone who can open your email can see it.

Your voice

The Mirror never records or keeps your voice. If you speak an answer, the words show up in a box so you can check them first.

  • On a phone, your keyboard’s microphone follows your phone’s own settings.
  • On a computer, your voice might be sent to a company such as Google, Microsoft or Apple to be typed. If so, we ask you first. You can say no and type instead. You can change this choice in Settings. If you say yes, that company’s rules apply to your voice.

Hands-free

  • An everyday answer saves itself after a 2-second pause, unless you touch the screen.
  • A private answer saves only when you say “save”.
  • Hands-free also asks you first, on phones too, before your voice goes to a company to be typed.

Email

Emails from the Mirror never contain health, legal or money details. They only say there is something new. You sign in to see it.

Today the Mirror sends no emails except your sign-in codes. Sign-in emails have a plain subject line that does not name the Mirror or housing.

Your choices
  • Change what you share at any time. It takes effect right away.
  • Download everything about you as a file. You can keep it, take it to someone else, or print a copy.
  • Ask us to delete your Mirror. We wait 30 days in case you change your mind. Then we delete it. We erase it or make it unreadable. Backup copies are removed automatically within 7 days after that. We keep a record that a deletion happened, without your information.
  • If you are in danger, tell your coordinator or call us. We will hide your Mirror from everyone right away while the 30 days run.

How we protect it
  • You sign in with a code we send to your email, so there is no password to steal. Keep your email private, because anyone who can open it can see the code.
  • Staff who work with people’s records also use an authenticator app (an app that gives a second sign-in code).
  • Each organization sees only its own people.
  • We store your records in a protected database. The rules about who can see what are enforced there, not only on the screen.

If your information is exposed

If someone who should not have your personal information gets it, we will tell you. We do this for any private topic, not only ID numbers.

  • We will tell you as soon as we can, and no later than 30 days after we find out.
  • We will use a safe way to reach you. Tell your coordinator or us how you want to be reached.
  • You can ask us in writing what information was taken.
  • One exception: if police ask us to wait so they can protect an investigation, we must wait until they say we can.

If our organization closes or changes

If 2nd Chance Housing closes, merges, or hands the Mirror to another group, we will tell you first. You will have time to download or delete your Mirror.

We will not give your information to anyone else unless they agree in writing to keep the promises on this page, or you agree.

Who can use the Mirror

The Mirror is for adults 18 and older. We do not create Mirror accounts for people under 18.

If you are under 18 and need help, you do not need a parent’s permission to contact us. Call or text 988, or call 911 in an emergency. You can also reach us at the number below. We will listen and help you find a program for young people.
