How it works

One conversation, at your pace

The Mirror is a conversation about your life, at your own pace. As you answer each question, a plan unfolds to help you become the person you want to be.

1. Eight ways of looking at your life

Each one is a few questions. You can start anywhere, skip anything and come back later. You never have to finish everything before you can get help.

  1. Turn on JavaScript to see the list.

2. Fifteen areas of life

Your answers are sorted into 15 life areas, so you and your coordinator can see what is going well, what you want to change and what would help. A blank answer never means you lack something. See all 15 areas.

3. Your Daily Number

What it costs to live one day, at three levels: getting by safely (survival), a steady life (stability), and the life you want (aspiration). A day is easier to plan around than a year. See how it works and try it.

Where and how you'd like to live

You can say how everyday activities go for you, what you would like, and which ways of living you want to learn about. It is never a diagnosis, and the Mirror never picks a place for you. See the living options.

4. Your plan

You pick the goals and the order. Each need becomes a step, with suggested programs and people who can help. Suggestions are marked "suggested" until someone confirms you qualify.

5. Speak or type

Every answer can be spoken. On a phone, tap the microphone on your keyboard. On a computer, press Speak. Questions and pages can be read aloud to you. The Mirror never records or keeps your voice.

On a computer, if your voice would be sent to a company such as Google, Microsoft or Apple to be typed, we ask you first. On a phone, the keyboard's microphone follows your phone's own settings. More about your voice.

What a coordinator can and can't see

  • They see what you choose to share with their organization, and nothing more.
  • Private topics (health, safety, legal matters, immigration, money details, ID papers) stay private unless you share them.
  • You can change what you share at any time, and you can see who looked at your Mirror.

More on the privacy page.

There is no score. The Mirror counts steps you've taken, never what a person is worth.
