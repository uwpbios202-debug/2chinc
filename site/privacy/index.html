Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how to ask us to see, fix or delete it.

Effective October 7, 2026

2nd Chance Housing Inc. ("we", "us") is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that provides rental housing in Chicago and Milwaukee. This policy covers this website, the online application, the resident portal, and the owner and association portals. We collect only what we need to house you, manage your lease and take your payments. We never sell your information.

What we collect

InformationWhen we collect it
Name, email, phone numberWhen you apply, sign in, contact us or sign a lease
Email, name, home size and the network (internet) address you connect fromWhen you join the waitlist. We use the network address only to stop automated sign-ups; it is deleted after 30 days.
Household members, including children's names and birth datesOn your application, because the lease must list everyone who lives in the unit
Your answer to "Tell us your plan" for keeping rent on track, typed or as an audio or video recordingOn your application
A photo of your driver's license or state IDOn your application, so staff can check the spelling of your name and birth date
Lease terms, unit, rent, deposits, charges and payment historyWhile you are a resident
Repair requests, portal messages, inspection notes and photosWhen you use the resident portal or we inspect the unit
Work-in-lieu-of-rent notes and photosWhen you do work in place of rent and it is recorded
Vehicle make, model, color and front and back photosWhen you register a vehicle, loaner or regular visitor for parking
Service or support animal informationWhen it applies to your lease
Your email and messageWhen you use the Contact form
Your signature, the time you signed, your IP address and your browser typeWhen you sign a lease online, as a record that you signed it
For property and association owners: contact details, units owned, dues, charges, payments and statementsWhile you use the owner or association portal

We do not run background or credit checks. We do not ask about your source of income, which fair housing laws protect in Milwaukee and Chicago. We no longer ask for a Social Security number; see "Older applications" below.

How we use it

  • To review your application and prepare and sign your lease
  • To bill rent and charges, record your payments and send receipts
  • To send notices the law or your lease requires, by email or mail
  • To handle repairs, inspections, move-in and move-out
  • To keep accounting and tax records

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or trade it.

Paying rent from your bank account

If you choose to pay rent from your bank account, the portal sends you to Stripe, our payment processor. You enter or connect your bank on Stripe's secure page, and Stripe may verify the account instantly with your bank. We never see or store your bank username, password, or account and routing numbers. We keep only Stripe's reference to the payment and its amount, date and status.

Paying by bank is optional. You can always pay another way, such as card, Zelle, PayPal, money order or check. You can remove a saved bank account at any time by emailing us.

Stripe handles your information under its own policy: Stripe Privacy Policy.

Our own bank account and Plaid

We use Plaid Inc. to connect our own business bank account, so we can see deposits as they arrive and mark your payment as received faster. Plaid is never used to connect to your bank. When you pay us by Zelle or bank transfer, the deposit record our bank shows (for example the sender's name and the amount) is what we use to match the payment to your account.

What other residents and owners can see

To manage parking, every resident and owner who signs in to the portal can see the registered vehicles for all units: the unit, make, model, color and vehicle photos. They cannot see your name, contact details, lease or payments. The plate number, VIN and mirror hanger number are seen only by the office and by the association's towing company (Always Towing & Recovery), which uses a private page to check which vehicles may park in each stall; it never sees names or contact details. A contractor assigned to a repair at your unit can see your name and phone number so they can arrange a time with you.

Voice dictation

Some forms, such as the Contact form and work notes, have a Speak button. It uses your web browser's built-in speech recognition, so the company that makes your browser (for example Google or Apple) may process the audio to turn it into text. If you prefer, type instead.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics services that follow you across websites. When you sign in, your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay signed in. The portal may also remember your email address and the bank you use for Zelle on that device, to save you typing. You can clear these at any time by signing out or clearing your browser's site data.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run our housing operations, and only what each one needs:

We may also share information when the law requires it (for example, a court order or an eviction filing), with a rent assistance or voucher program you have asked us to work with, or to protect someone's safety.

How we protect it

  • All connections to this website and our services are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Our database is encrypted when stored.
  • Residents and owners sign in with a one-time code sent to their email. Staff, field staff and lease agents also confirm with a code from an authenticator app.
  • Residents see their own lease, payments and messages, plus the shared vehicle list described above.
  • Office staff can see all records so they can run the properties. Field staff, inspectors and contractors see only the units and jobs assigned to them.
  • Bank account numbers are never stored in our systems.

If a security incident affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Payment and accounting records: 7 years, for tax and accounting.
  • Leases and notices: 7 years after the lease ends.
  • Applications started but never submitted: deleted after 7 days. If you saved your progress to finish later, after 45 days.
  • Applications that are declined or withdrawn: deleted after 90 days.
  • Waitlist: your email stays on the list until you take yourself off (every email has a link) or you never confirm it (deleted after 7 days). The network address recorded with each sign-up attempt is deleted after 30 days.
  • Driver's license and ID photos: removed once staff have checked them against your application.

Former residents keep portal access for 28 days after a voluntary move-out, so they can download their records.

Older applications

Before we stopped asking, some past applications collected a Social Security number. Those numbers are stored only in encrypted form, staff cannot view them, and we do not use them. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices and requests

You can ask us to show you the information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Email security@2ndchinc.org or write to us at the address below. We answer within 30 days. Some records, such as payment history, we must keep for the periods above even if you ask us to delete them; we will tell you if that applies.

Children

This website is for adults applying for or managing housing. We collect children's names and birth dates only from a parent or guardian, and only because the lease must list everyone in the household.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell current residents through the portal or by email.
