Pay association dues: 2nd Chance-owned units

ROCA1 dues for the units 2nd Chance Housing Inc owns, paid from 2nd Chance's bank account through ROCA1's secure Stripe page (bank account only). Pay any amount at any time, up to one year of dues per payment; to pay ahead further, make another payment. Anything beyond what is owed stays as a credit on the unit. This page is for the company's own units only. Tenants always pay their own rent, on their own device.
