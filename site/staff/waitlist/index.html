Waitlist

People sign up at https://2ndchinc.org/waitlist/ and get an email when a unit opens. Announce a unit below and everyone who fits gets one email. Email only; each email has a link to leave the list.

Rent ready

A unit shows as "Accepting applications" on https://2ndchinc.org/#units until you mark it rent ready here. Marking it rent ready emails everyone confirmed on the waitlist once, whatever size they asked for, and tells each person their number on the list. Number 1 (earliest sign-up) is emailed at once; everyone else 48 hours later, in waitlist order (setting waitlist_head_start_hours). Marking it not ready stops emails not yet sent.

Shared connections

Each connection (network address) may make 10 new sign-ups a day. Shelters, libraries and caseworker offices share one address, so once you have verified one, trust it: it then gets 200 a day. Addresses with 3 or more different emails in a week, or any blocked, are listed under "To review". Attempts are kept 30 days.
