Email a unit

Write to a unit's owner, its tenants, or both. It goes out from the office address, replies come to the office, and it is saved in the person's Communications history. You can format the text and attach files.

Formatting: **bold**, *italic*, a line starting with - for a bullet, [text](https://address) for a link. Web addresses become links by themselves.
