Housing Navigators run their own housing business on this system. Each Navigator's people, landlords and units are private to her: other Navigators never see them, the office sees everything here. After both agreements are dated on the Navigators tab she works on her own (no office acceptance or approval). Everyone shares the resource directory. Navigators sign in at https://2ndchinc.org/navigator/ .
