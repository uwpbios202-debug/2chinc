Staff and their units

Which staff login controls which units. A field staff member sees only the units ticked here. Tick or untick a unit to change it; it saves at once and is logged. Units marked 2CH are owned by 2nd Chance Housing Inc: office staff, and field staff assigned to one of them, can pay its association dues at Pay dues. Office staff see every unit anyway.
