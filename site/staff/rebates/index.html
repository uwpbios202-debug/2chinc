Menards receipts & rebates

Upload a Menards receipt and it is read for you. Check the rebate amounts, then print the rebate claim and the envelope, and track each rebate until the check comes.

1. Upload a receipt

Take a photo of the receipt, or choose the photo or PDF.
A long receipt can be several photos of the same purchase (up to 6). Include the rebate receipt if Menards printed one.

Photo saved. Take the next rebate photo?

    2. Rebates to mail

    Rebates going to the same address can share one envelope. Tick the ones going in this envelope, print the claim and the envelope, put the original rebate receipts in (no staples), then press Mark mailed.

    3. Waiting for the rebate check

    Menards pays rebates as a merchandise credit check, good in Menards stores. Mark each one when it comes.

    Track in-store purchases in the Menards account
    1. Sign in to the 2nd Chance Menards account (info@2ndchinc.org) at https://www.menards.com/main/accountoverview.html. Preferred store: Milwaukee, 8110 W Brown Deer Rd.
    2. Under Wallet → Payment Methods, add every card 2nd Chance pays with at Menards. Receipts are looked up by card, so a purchase only shows up if its card is saved there.
    3. After each store trip: Purchase History → Lookup Receipts (https://www.menards.com/main/receiptLookup.html), pick the card, tick the purchase, Download receipt, and upload the PDF above. The paper rebate receipt is still the one that goes in the envelope.
    4. Rebate offers and forms: https://www.menards.com/main/rebates.html.
