Problem reports

What tenants send with the "Report a problem" button. Fix the tenant's email or phone here, issue a new registration code, reply, then mark it resolved.

Registration: who has signed in?

Everyone on an active lease. "Registered" means they used the code on their notice. "Signed in" is the last time they signed in to the portal.

Email delivery: the "register your account" email

One row per tenant. Opened is only a hint (some phones hide it); Registered and Last sign-in are the real proof they read it. A bounced email is red: fix the address (Fix their contact info above, or here), then send it again.
