Charge disputes

Tenants question charges on their rent ledger, owners on their association account. Answer to explain (they can reply), deny with the reason the charge stands, or credit some or all of it. A credit is a new line on the same ledger, with a note citing the dispute; the original charge is never changed. A dispute doesn't pause late fees or notices; waive or credit those here if they should go.
