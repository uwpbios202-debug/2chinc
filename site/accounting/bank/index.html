Bank deposits

The bank feed pulls each deposit and posts it to the right ledger by itself: rent for tenants, dues for owners, kept in their own bank accounts. You only see a deposit here when it can't tell who it belongs to. Tell it once and every later payment from that person posts on its own.

Bank alerts and confirmations

Alert emails (Zelle, Cash App, PayPal) and extra copies of a deposit. They never post money; they mark a reported payment as "bank alert seen" and are linked once the bank's own record posts.
