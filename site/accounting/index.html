Accounting

Two separate banking systems. Tenant money and owner money go to different bank accounts and must never be mixed. They share only the list of account types.

Tenant side

Rent, deposits, fees and violations. Paid to 2nd Chance Housing's account.

Owner side

Association dues and assessments. Paid to the owners' (ROCA1) account.

Account types

Shared chart of accounts: maintenance, repairs, violations and more.

Reports

Printable reports for the accountant: income by GL, receipts, rent roll, aging, deposits, dues, land contract.

Housing assistance

Vouchers and agency-paid rent: the agency portion, the tenant portion, and what each agency owes.

Charge questions

Tenants and owners questioning a charge: answer, deny with a reason, or credit it.
