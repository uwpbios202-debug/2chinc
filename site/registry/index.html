Registry information

Where to find Wisconsin sex offender registry information.

Notice under Wis. Stat. § 704.50

You can get registry information from the Wisconsin Department of Corrections (DOC).

Website: wistaysafe.gov (the Wisconsin Public Sex Offender Registry, searchable by name)

2nd Chance Housing does not search the registry for applicants. If you are a prospective renter and want to know whether a particular person is required to register, the DOC is where to find out. If we have actual knowledge about a person you ask about, we will tell you.

The registry is not a complete record of anyone’s history, and the DOC cannot guarantee that its information is accurate. The Legislature does not intend the information to be used to injure, harass, or commit a crime against anyone listed, their families, or their employers.

Questions: info@2ndchinc.org or (262) 236-5443.
