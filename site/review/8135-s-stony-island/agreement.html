  • Order of payment: operating costs are listed third, after build-out. Many properties cannot operate if insurance and utilities wait behind repayment of build-out costs, so consider paying operations first or setting a reserve. This follows the instruction as given.
  • The step 5(d) and step 6 rule that no Owner is paid until all Encumbrances are zero is as instructed. Confirm whether "paid" also covers payments to Owners for managing, repaying their build-out costs, or loans they made.
  • Co-ownership as tenants in common lets any Owner ask a court to force a sale (partition). Consider whether to waive that, or whether an LLC holding title would be simpler for three parties.
  • Check Illinois and Cook County transfer tax, the transfer declaration and any exemptions for the new deed, and whether the nonprofit status of 2nd Chance affects ownership shares or reporting.
  • Consider an agreement signed before the new deed, and a recorded memorandum so a later buyer or lender has notice.
  • Define who counts as having "paid" a build-out cost, how costs are approved, and what happens if costs are never repaid.
