8135 S Stony Island Ave: Budget Estimate (materials and labor)

Prepared 10/29/2026 for the owner's team by 2nd Chance Housing Inc. Planning numbers for budgeting, not a bid. Read the "How reliable" box before using any figure.

Materials and labor, before contingency: $161,575 to $293,650. With a 15% contingency: $185,811 to $337,698.
Plus kitchen equipment, second-hand and already owned: $8,000. Total: $193,811 to $345,698.
Uses the team's real figures for structure and envelope, finishes and sound, kitchen building materials, electrical and plumbing; planning estimates for the other sections. Design, permits, demolition, utility connection fees, tree clearing, freight and sales tax are not included.

How reliable these numbers are

  • Weak sources. Most published cost pages are contractor marketing and many disagree with each other. I could not find Chicago-specific supplier quotes. Treat the estimated sections as a first budget. Materials and delivery are sourced through 2nd Chance Housing's Home Depot relationship, so Home Depot quotes should replace the estimated prices.
  • Basis column: S = a published material price. I = a published installed price that I cut down to a materials share (the share is my assumption, shown in the note). A = my own allowance because no usable published price was found. About 69% of the lines are allowances.
  • Quantities are guesses until the building is measured: first floor 4,300 sq ft (kitchen about 1,200 sq ft in the garage), second floor 1,500, basement 1,500, rooftop party area 2,000, parking lot 3,000, SIP wall area 4,800 gross, roof panels 1,650. Change any quantity and the totals change in proportion.
  • Roof loads. Panels that carry a crowded party deck need engineering. SIP roof panels are priced only over the second floor. The deck over the first floor is priced as a built-up steel and concrete system.
  • Existing walls. If the exterior masonry is sound, the SIP quantity may drop. If the walls are not sound, it may rise.
